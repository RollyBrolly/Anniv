A little note for you

My love---my dear Ella,

1 year, 365 days, 8,760 hours, 525,600 minutes and 31,536,000 seconds. A whole year has passed yet despite all the things that happened-the things we went through, you're still the person I choose, I yearn for, I want---

I love.

It all started in my freshmen's orientation week. The crowd was roaring with excitement. Everyone pushed up against each other in the blazing 8 AM heat at the time. With the host of the event hyping the crowd up, giving her welcoming remarks yet I can't help but be fixated on this one person at the back. She was pretty. She was beautiful. She was so damned gorgeous that everything and everyone felt silent at the moment as I silently admired this girl from a distance. It's funny-our campus guide was supposed to help me find my way around the university, but somehow, I ended up searching for her instead. Turns out, she was the only orientation I really needed.

The sole person I've spent nearly 8 months trying to find around campus is now at my bed, sleeping beside me, drooling on my back (as of writing this letter). We've been through hell and back together, but still we choose each other despite it all. Perhaps that's what cemented this relationship to be ours and ours only as we continuously choose each other in the storm. My love for you is unwavering, still with that warm spark within with each day being kindled turning into a blazing affection and intimacy within.

To where you are is where I am, and I wouldn't even dare to say that you will be loved simply because I'm here, cause even in my absence, my love for you will not leave. I will love you always in all ways---in my presence, in my absence, in our quiet moments of cuddling, in our cold and messy arguments, in moments where we share different lives, and even the distance (hopefully not) that may possibly come. My love for you will not falter. Find me in serendipity, in moments where you feel joy, in moments where you feel lost, in moments where you eat Cheezy or Chicken Joo, in moments where you play on our Minecraft server, in moments where life is unexpectedly kind to you, in moments where everything is all in place, in moments where you feel cared for.

When those moments come, recognize me within them. I don't know why I wrote this as if I'm dead pero maganda kasi yung pagkasulat ko sa simula nung previous paragraph kaya tinuloy tuloy kona (best regards, Maxwell Pasig Next Gen). Kidding aside, perhaps that's how I will always be with you despite my absence, not necessarily physically there but letting the good in life find its path to you.

Remember that if love is a choice we make again and again and again, then let me spend my lifetime choosing you---choosing us. I love you so much my dearest Ella.

Always and will always be yours, Bryle
